      Video by RailCam Singapore (@railcamsg) on YouTube. The stream uses mobile data and stops when you leave this tab.

      How this works. In the MRT Tracker phone app, trains and next-train times follow LTA's official train timetable, the same published schedule journey planners use, including public holidays. Where the timetable isn't available, they're estimated from typical frequencies and the operators' own station-to-station travel times. Either way, they show the schedule, so delays don't appear on the map; disruption notices from LTA appear under Updates.
